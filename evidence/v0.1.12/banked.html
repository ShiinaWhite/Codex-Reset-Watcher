codex-reset --faq

Banked reset questions, answered

Does receiving a banked reset refresh my usage?

No. The reset is saved until you redeem it. A grant and a quota refresh are separate events.

What changes when I redeem a full banked reset?

Both the 5-hour and weekly usage windows reset, and the weekly reset date moves to roughly seven days after redemption.

Do all banked resets expire after 30 days?

Do not generalize that rule. Referral banked resets normally use 30 days unless the offer says otherwise; other grants use the expiry shown by the offer or account.

Can this page see how many resets I have?

No. It stores only the next or earliest expiry you enter in this browser and never reads your OpenAI account.

Should I spend it now?

That depends on your remaining weekly usage, natural rollover, expiry, and whether 5-hour usage is the bottleneck. The calculator models only the weekly trade-off.
